# Privacy Policy — Flint

**Last updated:** March 14, 2026

## What Flint does
Flint is a Chrome extension that reads the text you type in Claude.ai and sends it to our server to analyze and score it.

## What data we collect
- **Prompt text**: The text you type into Claude.ai is sent to our backend (flint-backend-two.vercel.app) for analysis. We do **not** store this text after returning the result. It is processed and discarded immediately.
- **No personal data**: We do not collect your name, email, IP address, or any identifying information.
- **No browsing history**: We only activate on claude.ai. We do not read any other tabs or websites.

## What we don't do
- We do not sell your data
- We do not store your prompts
- We do not share anything with third parties
- We do not require an account or login

## Third-party services
Flint uses **Groq** (groq.com) to process prompt analysis. Prompts are sent to Groq's API and are subject to [Groq's privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/). Groq does not retain prompt data for training purposes.

## Local storage
Flint stores your usage statistics (number of prompts analyzed, scores) locally in Chrome storage on your device. This data never leaves your browser.
