## Name

## Short Description (132 chars max)
Score and improve your Claude prompts instantly. Get a 1–10 rating and one-click rewrites — so Claude actually gets what you want.

## Detailed Description
**Stop wasting Claude credits on prompts that miss the mark.**

Flint sits on Claude.ai and watches your prompts in real time. The moment you type something, it scores it from 1–10 and tells you exactly what to fix — in plain English, no jargon.

**How it works:**
1. Type a prompt in Claude
2. Flint shows your score instantly (1–10)
3. See the one thing that would make it better
4. Hit "Improve it" — Flint rewrites your prompt for you
5. Repeat until you hit 8+ and Claude actually nails it

**Why it matters:**
Most people type vague prompts like "fix the bug" or "build me an app" and get mediocre results. Flint turns those into detailed, specific prompts that get 10x better responses — without you needing to know anything about "prompt engineering."

**Built for:**
- Vibe coders using Claude to build apps
- Designers who want better outputs from AI
- Anyone who's frustrated that Claude "doesn't get it"

**No account required. No API key. Just install and go.**
